Privacy Policy — Guess Breakers

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This website

This website uses no cookies or tracking tools. GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

Our YouTube channel

Guess Breakers is a YouTube channel made for kids. We never collect personal information from children. YouTube disables comments on made-for-kids content. We do not run contests or ask children to contact us.

Guess Breakers tools

“Guess Breakers tools” is a Google OAuth app used only by the channel owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It uploads videos to that channel and reads its aggregated analytics.

The app requests these Google API scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl — upload caption tracks for the owner’s videos.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — read channel and video metadata for the owner's channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly — read aggregated channel analytics.

Information accessed includes channel and video metadata and aggregated, anonymous statistics such as views, watch time, audience retention and subscriber counts. The app stores an OAuth refresh token, upload records and analytics reports privately in the owner’s local project and private GitHub repository. Analytics reports are not used for advertising or to train AI models.

The owner keeps analytics reports while running the channel and can delete them at any time. Revoking access (below) stops the app from accessing any further data.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at Google Account permissions.
