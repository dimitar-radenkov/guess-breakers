Guess Breakers makes short animated science mysteries for curious kids around 7–10. Each episode invites a guess, tests an idea, and follows the evidence. Every answer is backed by a real source listed under the video. Demonstrations and reconstructions are labelled honestly, and it’s okay when a guess breaks.

An otter, a girl, a robot and a boy notice clues, compare ideas, measure and connect discoveries to everyday life. New mysteries regularly, for families and classrooms to enjoy together.
